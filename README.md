# THIQA - ثقة

## About the Project
THIQA is a community-based platform designed
to help people in Yemen verify the credibility
of online sellers, phone numbers, digital wallets,
and online stores.

## Problem
Online fraud is increasing in Yemen due to the
lack of reliable platforms for verifying online
transactions and identifying suspicious entities.

## Main Features
- Search and verify identifiers.
- Submit fraud reports with evidence.
- Admin review and moderation.
- Display verified reports and information.

## Target Users
- Online buyers.
- Honest online sellers.
- Platform administrators.
